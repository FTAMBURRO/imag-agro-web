---
name: Self-contained public site assets
description: IMAG Agro should remain usable without external font or image dependencies until official brand assets are supplied.
---

The public site should keep its initial presentation self-contained with system fonts and CSS/SVG field treatments. Official photography, logos, and other brand files can replace those treatments only after licensing and source details are confirmed.

**Why:** The company brief explicitly prohibits broken or unlicensed external assets and the project currently has no official media package.

**How to apply:** Keep asset replacement instructions in `ASSETS_NEEDED.md` and attribution details in `ATTRIBUTION.md`; do not reintroduce runtime stock URLs or remote font imports.
